# Why WitnessOps exists

WitnessOps exists to make consequential AI and security work independently verifiable. A customer should be able to hand a result to another engineer who can reconstruct what was authorized, what actually changed, which evidence supports that conclusion, and which questions remain open.

**Easy in the foreground. Reconstructable in the background.** A buyer should be able to describe the action they are concerned about, agree a scope and understand the result. Evidence should be available when someone needs to check or challenge it. The [accepted usability principle](docs/product-decisions/WITNESSOPS_USABILITY_FIRST_INVISIBLE_PROOF_PRINCIPLE_V1.md) governs this experience.

## Who the work serves

Our immediate commercial objective is acquiring and successfully serving real customers. A security or engineering lead buys a bounded assessment to decide what to fix. A workspace member uses the product to retain evidence and share a result with a named recipient. An Academy learner needs practice and feedback. Supporting infrastructure earns its place when it helps one of those people complete a task.

- Start with the customer's problem. Expand infrastructure when an existing capability cannot meet a demonstrated need.
- Use familiar buying language and a short path from enquiry to agreement. Create a category only when existing language fails the customer.
- Obtain human authority for the exact consequential action before execution.
- Match each claim to evidence. Report a missing observation or a limit to reproduction alongside the finding.
- Judge a deliverable by whether another person can use and reproduce the result. A dashboard is useful only when it helps them do that.
