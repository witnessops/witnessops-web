# Public editorial routes

Before drafting or changing an article, read repository-root `docs/editorial/WITNESSOPS_EDITORIAL_STYLE.md` and `docs/editorial/ARTICLE_CONTRACT.md`. These scoped instructions supplement root/app rules; they do not authorize publication.

Use the shared editorial shell and metadata registry. Preserve existing canonical URLs and approved text unless rewriting is explicitly requested. Do not invent author experience or evidence. `/research` is the discovery index; do not duplicate article bodies there.
