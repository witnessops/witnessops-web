WitnessOps Academy

WitnessOps Academy is the school for the Sovereign tools. Short lessons: the teacher explains one idea, shows a worked example, reads your attempt, and helps you try again. Coaching, not assessment. No formal award is issued in the pilot.

The four tracks

I · Work with AI

coming soon

For people who use AI tools in their daily work. You learn to give AI a job it can actually do, read what comes back, and know when to stop trusting the summary and open the source. Lessons are not written yet.

II · Build agents

coming soon

For people who build or configure AI agents. You learn to give an agent a clear job, the right tools and a definition of "done" that something other than the agent can check. Lessons are not written yet. Uses Sovereign Tasks.

III · Operate safely

coming soon

For people who run agents with real access and permissions. You learn to give an agent only the access the job needs, keep a record of what it touched, and take the access back when the job ends. Lessons are not written yet. Uses Sovereign Linux boxes and scoped access.

IV · Verify results

pilot open, by invitation

For anyone who has to decide whether an AI's report is true. Two pilot lessons with a teacher are live: Report or evidence? and Same record, wrong scope? You sort each claim into evidenced, only claimed, or contradicted, then state what the evidence does not prove.
