I · Work with AI

AI can do real work for you. You still decide what counts as done.

Status: coming soon · lessons not written yet

Who it's for

People who use AI tools in their daily work: writing, research, summaries, planning. No coding needed.

The problem it solves

AI answers sound finished even when they aren't. A clean summary can skip the one line that mattered, and a confident answer can rest on a source nobody opened.

What you'll be able to do

  • Write a request with a clear job, the inputs, and what "done" looks like.
  • Split an AI answer into single claims you can check.
  • Tell which parts came from a source and which the AI filled in.
  • Decide when to open the source instead of trusting the summary.

Lesson outline

  1. Give it a job, not a wish
  2. One answer, many claims
  3. Where did that come from?
  4. When to stop and check

What it isn't

Not a prompt-tricks list. Not a tour of one product. Not a course on how AI models work inside.

Track I is being written. Start with Track IV, Verify results, which is open now in pilot.
