III · Operate safely

Give an agent the keys it needs for this job, and get them back after.

Status: coming soon · lessons not written yet

Who it's for

People who run agents with real access and permissions: to systems, accounts, files or money.

The problem it solves

Access granted for one job tends to stay. When an agent can do more than the task needs, a mistake reaches further, and afterwards nobody can say exactly what it touched.

What you'll be able to do

  • Scope access to one job: what, where, and for how long.
  • Decide which actions need a person's explicit approval first.
  • Keep a record of what the agent touched while it worked.
  • Take access back when the job ends, and confirm it is gone.

Lesson outline

  1. Least access for this job
  2. Actions that wait for a yes
  3. A record of what it touched
  4. Closing the job: revoke and confirm

Sovereign tools

Uses Sovereign Linux boxes and scoped access in the lessons.

What it isn't

Not a full security course. Not a compliance checklist. Not a promise that an agent can be made risk-free.

Track III is being written. Track IV, Verify results, is open now in pilot.
