III · How a lesson works

Explain, show, try, feedback, retry

  1. IThe teacher explains one idea in plain words.
  2. IIA worked example. The teacher does it once, slowly.
  3. IIIYour turn. A short exercise in your own words.
  4. IVFeedback. What was right, what to do again, then a retry.
IV · In the pilot

What to expect

Coaching, not assessment. In the pilot you send your answer to the teacher in an existing conversation and paste the reply back into the lesson by hand. There are no accounts and nothing is saved by this page. No certificate is issued in the pilot.
