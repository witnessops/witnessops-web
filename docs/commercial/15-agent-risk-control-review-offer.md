**Commercial status:** Primary homepage offer as of 2026-08-26.

**Core positioning:** Agents act. WitnessOps proves.

**Required descriptor:** A focused review of one agentic or automated workflow.

## Buyer promise

Bring one agentic workflow. WitnessOps maps the workflow, identifies authority and evidence gaps, defines the receipt schema, and produces a clearly labelled sample proof bundle showing how the action can be approved, executed, evidenced, and verified.

## Best for

Security teams, platform teams, compliance teams, MSSPs, and AI automation teams that are letting agents or automations touch sensitive systems.

## Deliverables

- workflow map
- agent and tool permission model
- approval and policy gap analysis
- evidence requirements
- receipt schema
- sample proof bundle
- verifier path
- control recommendations

## Commercial contract

- From €1,500.
- One named agentic or automated workflow.
- Exact scope, fee, timing, authority, and evidence handling are confirmed during a non-secret fit check.
- Customer evidence is accepted only after scope and handling are agreed.
- There is no self-serve checkout and no claim that submitting the fit check starts a review.

## Outcome

The buyer knows whether the workflow can be defended in an audit, customer review, or incident investigation, and what proof or controls remain missing.

## Verification boundary

A receipt proves only what its named verifier and referenced evidence support. It does not certify that the agent was correct, safe, compliant, or complete. The sample proof bundle demonstrates the proposed receipt and verifier path; it is not customer evidence and does not claim the controls have been deployed in production.
