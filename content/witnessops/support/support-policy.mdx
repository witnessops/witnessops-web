# Support Policy

WitnessOps support runs through the public support form and the shared public contact route. The public form routes to email follow-up; it does not write an admin queue entry.

## Support channels

- Public support form: [/support](/support)
- General contact fallback: [engage@mail.witnessops.com](mailto:engage@mail.witnessops.com?subject=WitnessOps%20request)

For a new bounded security or operational review, use [Start a review](/review/request). Support email is for product help, access issues, and verifier questions — not a substitute for the review request path.

Do not send passwords, private keys, API keys, recovery codes, session tokens, or other secrets.

Use support for product questions, verification help, access issues, and policy-gate questions that are not security incidents.

## Response target

General support aims for a first response within 1 business day. This is the current public target, not a 24/7 guarantee.

## Security issues

Report vulnerabilities through responsible disclosure at [security@witnessops.com](mailto:security@witnessops.com). The broader disclosure policy lives at [Security](/security). Security reports do not go through the normal support channel.

## Limits

WitnessOps does not promise 24/7 live support, instant resolution, or emergency incident response through the support mailbox.
