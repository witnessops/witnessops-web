# Your first observation

## Before you begin

Create a [free account](/docs/getting-started). Verify your email and create your own workspace, or accept an Owner’s invitation to an existing workspace. The browser app needs no installation. You need the **Owner** or **Contributor** role to add an asset and start a supported check; a **Viewer** can inspect accessible workspace data.

Want a public result without an account? Use [Free check](/check) for a hostname you own or are authorized to check. Its result and download do not require registration. That snapshot is not automatically imported into the app.

## Add and check a hostname

1. Sign in to [the app](https://app.witnessops.com/login) and confirm the workspace and role.
2. Open **Assets**, choose **Add asset**, and enter the public hostname you are authorized to check.
3. Open the asset. Review the check's scope and authorization controls before starting it. Adding an asset does not establish ownership or permission to collect.
4. Start the supported observation only after confirming authorization. Account creation and CLI login do not start a check.
5. Read the completed observation and its limitations. An unresolved result is not a pass. A failed or incomplete run is not evidence that a system is secure.

Checks are manually initiated; do not assume scheduled monitoring. Linux server checks and signed Local Audit imports have separate collection, scope and trust requirements. Do not use privileged collection commands merely to test login.

## Find the result again

Open **Assets** for the asset's observation history or **Reports** for saved reports. Use **View report** from an observation. A workspace without saved observations has no reports yet. A new observation does not silently rewrite the previous completed source snapshot.
