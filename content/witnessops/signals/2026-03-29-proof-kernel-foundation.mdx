At the time of this milestone, the proof design lane included DSSE, RFC 3161
binding, in-toto alignment, receipt chaining, bundle packaging, and offline
verification flow.

Current contract note (2026-08-24): the public `/verify` surface is receipt-only.
For the Public Exposure Review profile it checks bounded receipt structure and
preserves an `indeterminate` result because evidence bytes, workflow execution,
signer authorization, and cryptographic trust inputs are not independently
checked there. The canonical receipt/profile pair is
`witnessops.receipt.v0` plus `witnessops.verification_context.v1`.
